# Vision

## Problem

Following the news means checking many sites, reading the same story five
times, and wading through clickbait, filler, and outrage bait to find the few
things that actually matter.

## Goal

A personal app that collects news from sources I choose, throws out the noise,
merges duplicate coverage into single stories, and gives me one short daily
digest I can read in a few minutes. Stories I care about can be tracked over
time so I see what changed, not the whole story again.

## What counts as "bullshit" (filtered out or ranked down)

- Duplicates: the same story from several outlets → merged into one entry
- Clickbait headlines with no substance behind them
- Filler: listicles, "you won't believe", celebrity gossip (unless opted in)
- Rehashes with no new information since the last digest
- Topics I have explicitly muted

## Principles

- **Personal first.** One user, runs locally, no accounts or cloud required.
- **Sources are explicit.** I decide what goes in; nothing is pulled from an opaque feed.
- **Transparent filtering.** Every dropped item has a stated reason I can inspect.
- **Short output.** The digest is the product; it must stay readable in under 10 minutes.
- **Link to originals.** Summaries never replace the source; every item links out.

## Success looks like

- I read one digest per day instead of opening several news sites.
- Same story from different outlets appears once.
- I rarely see something I'd call noise, and I can see why things were filtered.
